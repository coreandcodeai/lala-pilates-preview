FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Everything people ask before their first class at La La Pilates — and a few things they ask after. Can't find your answer? Email info@lalapilates.co.uk.

Getting started

Is reformer Pilates suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. We run dedicated Beginner Group Classes, and every class is capped at four people so your instructor can set your springs, straps and pace personally. Private sessions are also available if you'd rather start one-to-one.

What's the difference between reformer and mat Pilates?

Mat Pilates uses your body weight on the floor; reformer Pilates uses a sliding carriage and springs. The springs can both support you and challenge you, which makes the reformer especially good for beginners and for targeting specific muscles precisely.

What should I wear and bring?

Leggings or shorts and a fitted top you can move in — nothing baggy that could catch in the springs. Grip socks are ideal for keeping your feet steady; otherwise just bring yourself and a water bottle.

How early should I arrive?

About ten minutes before your first class. That gives us time to set your reformer up, talk through any injuries and show you the machine before the class starts.

Do I need to be fit or flexible already?

No. The reformer meets you where you are — springs and settings adjust every exercise to your level. Strength and flexibility are what you build here, not what you need to arrive with.

The studio

There's on-street parking on Elmbank Street and Charing Cross Car Park is a 2-minute walk (check parking charges). Charing Cross Station is 3 minutes on foot, Glasgow Central and Queen Street are a short walk, and Cowcaddens and St George's Cross Subway stations are close by.

How many people are in a class?

Four, maximum — the studio has four Align-Pilates reformers and that's the whole room. It keeps every class personal.

Health & injuries

Is Pilates good for back pain?

Reformer Pilates is widely used to build the deep core and postural strength that supports the spine, and many members join on a physiotherapist's advice. Tell us about any pain when you book and we'll adapt every exercise.

I have an injury — can I still come?

Usually yes, with adaptations. Mention the injury when you book and arrive a little early so we can talk it through. For recent injuries or rehab, a private session is often the best starting point.

Can I do reformer Pilates while pregnant?

Please get in touch before booking. Depending on your stage, your experience and your midwife or GP's advice, we'll recommend the safest option — often a private session where everything can be tailored to you.
